Data Transparency

Privacy Policy

Your financial privacy is our utmost priority. Learn how we safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide you with seamless expense tracking services. This includes account credentials (username, email, encrypted password hash) and transaction details entered voluntarily by you (date, amount, category, note, payment type).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your expense data is used exclusively to generate your personal dashboard, track category budgets, calculate daily spending alerts, and produce CSV export files. We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records to any third party.

3. Data Security & Storage

All database connections are secured via SSL/TLS encryption. Sensitive session cookies and CSRF tokens are enforced across all authenticated endpoints to prevent unauthorized access and data tampering.

4. Data Export & Account Deletion

You maintain 100% control of your personal records. You can download a complete CSV copy of your transactions at any time or request account and data removal by contacting us.

5. Contact Our Privacy Team

For privacy inquiries or data rights requests, email us directly at kharchagraph@gmail.com.
